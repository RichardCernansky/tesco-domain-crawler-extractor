REGEX NOTES:
0. flags - I - case insensitive, S - . matches also '\n'
1. PRICE REGEX
(?:(?:.*?Aldi Price Match){2}|.*?Reviews?)
Non-capturing (?:) group with an OR:
(?:.*?Aldi Price Match){2} → advance lazily through text until you’ve passed the second “Aldi Price Match”.
.*?Reviews? → or advance to “Review”/“Reviews”.
Effect: position the engine after your chosen anchor.
.*?>
Lazily consume characters up to the next > (the end of the next start tag), so the next thing is the tag’s inner text.
\s*
Optional whitespace right after >.
[^<]*
Any inner text until the next < (stays inside this text node).
([£])
Group 1: capture the currency symbol £.
(Change to ([£€]) if you want euros too.)
(?:\s|&nbsp;)*
Allow spaces or &nbsp; between the symbol and the number.
([0-9]+(?:\.[0-9]{1,2})?)
Group 2: capture the numeric price: digits, optional decimal point + 1–2 digits.
<
Require that we’re still before the next tag start, keeping the match inside the same text node.
